What's actually coming out of a Bradenton tap.

Manatee County blends deep Floridan Aquifer groundwater with Lake Manatee reservoir water, then disinfects it with chlorine. Great for killing germs. Less great for your skin, your pipes and your coffee. Tap a callout to read the detail.

DETAIL 1 Level · High

Hard water (calcium & magnesium)

Floridan Aquifer water picks up dissolved limestone on its way to your tap. That's the white crust on your faucets, the film on your glassware, and the reason your water heater works harder every year.

  • Scale in water heaters & appliances
  • Dry skin, dull hair
  • Up to 2× more soap & detergent
Specified remedyHigh-efficiency softener or salt-free conditioner

03Section A-A · Flow left to right

Follow one drop through the system.

STN 1STN 2STN 3STN 4STN 5
Detail 1 / Sediment capture

Grit stops at the door.

Sand, silt, rust and construction grit are caught in a multi-layer sediment stage so nothing downstream, including your appliances, has to deal with them.

Detail 2 / Catalytic carbon

Where the taste changes.

Catalytic carbon pulls out chlorine, chloramine, trihalomethanes and the pool smell. This stage alone is why the first shower feels different.

Detail 3 / Softening

Scale stops forming.

Calcium and magnesium are exchanged out so crust stops building on fixtures and the water heater. Softer skin, brighter laundry.

Detail 4 / UV sterilization

No chemicals, no germs.

Ultraviolet light neutralizes bacteria, viruses and cysts. Essential for well water, smart for everyone.

Detail 5 / Reverse osmosis

Drinking water to the molecule.

An under-sink RO stage removes PFAS, lead, arsenic and dissolved solids, then adds minerals back for taste.
