What's actually in Bradenton water

Every drop tells a story.
Tap one to read it.

Manatee County blends deep Floridan Aquifer groundwater with Lake Manatee reservoir water, then disinfects it with chlorine. That's great for killing germs. It's less great for your skin, your pipes, and your coffee.

Level: High

Hard water (calcium & magnesium)

Floridan Aquifer water picks up dissolved limestone on its way to your tap. That's the white crust on your faucets, the film on your glassware, and the reason your water heater works harder every year.

  • Scale in water heaters & appliances
  • Dry skin, dull hair
  • Up to 2× more soap & detergent
Our fixHigh-efficiency softener or salt-free conditioner

Figures are regional estimates for illustration. Your free in-home test gives you the real numbers for your tap.

Stage 01

Sediment capture

Sand, silt, rust and grit are stopped at the door. A multi-layer sediment stage protects everything downstream, including your appliances.

Stage 02

Catalytic carbon

High-grade catalytic carbon pulls out chlorine, chloramine, trihalomethanes and the pool smell. This is where the taste changes.

Stage 03

Softening & conditioning

Calcium and magnesium are neutralized so scale stops forming. Softer skin, brighter laundry, and a water heater that lasts.

Stage 04

UV sterilization

Ultraviolet light neutralizes bacteria, viruses and cysts without chemicals. Essential for well water, smart for everyone.

Stage 05

Reverse osmosis at the tap

An under-sink RO stage polishes your drinking water to near-pure, removing PFAS, lead, arsenic and dissolved solids down to the molecule.
